What is Linear Regression?

Linear regression is the first pit stop we will be taking on this Machine Learning journey. This supervised Machine Learning model allows us to predict an output (dependant variable). The equation that is used for this algorithm is: \( y = \beta_1 x_1 + \beta_0 + \epsilon\). Where \( y\) is the dependant variable. \( \beta_1\) is the slope of the model. \( x_1\) is the independant variable. \( \beta_0\) is the y-intercept of the model. And finally \( \epsilon\) is the loss of the model; this will be explained later on.
