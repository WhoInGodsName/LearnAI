What does “same feather” mean?

K-means tries to group similar data points together. You choose K, the number of clusters, and the algorithm searches for K centres that represent the groups. Each point belongs to the cluster with the nearest centre.

After assigning the points, K-means moves each centre to the average position of the points assigned to it. It repeats these two steps until the assignments stop changing much.

How does the elbow graph help?

The elbow graph plots the within-cluster sum of squares (WCSS) for each value of K. WCSS measures how far points are from their assigned centres, so it always gets smaller as more clusters are added. Look for the bend where the improvement starts to level off: beyond that point, extra clusters explain much less.
